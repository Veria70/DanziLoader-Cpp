# M4.7a: submissions sent ahead (the relay, on by default: late stage 3).
# A queue's submission goes to the card without a wait; the relay's thread
# lands it (copies of title memory done at submission, bottom-of-pipe

# settles), and polled after a present. Waits for memory values across
# queues are met by a flight's bottom-of-pipe write without a wait for the
