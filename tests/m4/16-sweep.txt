# M4.11: the sweep. A texture the title fills (its host copy and staging
# buffer) and a window of title memory outside buffers (a root UAV into it)
# are used, then left idle past the sweep's times (10.5 s, 40 frames); a
# submission with nothing in the air runs the sweep, which lets them go;
# then both are used again: made anew from title memory, the results as
# before. Every result read back and hashed.

set_pipeline l p1

dispatch l 1 1 1
list_close l
execute q l
